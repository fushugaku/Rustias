Rustias

A native Rust synthesizer for your DAW. Requires macOS 12 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel.

Choose the formats you use with Customize:

  • Audio Unit — Logic Pro, GarageBand
  • VST3 — Ableton Live, Cubase, REAPER
  • CLAP — Bitwig Studio, REAPER

Plugins install for your account into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/. Existing versions are updated. An administrator password is not required.

Quit your DAW before installing, then reopen it and rescan plugins.
